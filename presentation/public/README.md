# Slide assets

Drop the three logo images here with these **exact filenames** (they are referenced
by the title slide):

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `msa-university.png` | MSA University wreath logo ("MSA UNIVERSITY · since 1996") |
| `msa-seal.png`       | Faculty of Computer Science circular seal |
| `greenwich.png`      | University of Greenwich (London \| Egypt) logo |

PNG with transparent or white background works best. Anything in `public/` is served
from the site root, e.g. `public/greenwich.png` → `/greenwich.png`.
